About Me

Hello! My name is Maxine Muchinda, and I am a Computer Science student at Mulungushi University. I am passionate about technology and the way the web connects people, ideas, and opportunities across the world. You can reach me on 0765692555 or by email at maxwellmuchinda@gmail.com. HTML

Activity 1: My First Web Page

I built my first page with headings, paragraphs, lists and links. I learned how semantic tags such as header, nav, main and footer give a page a clear structure.

CSS

Activity 2: Styled Personal Website
